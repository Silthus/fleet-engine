# Standing rules

Rules I give that decide for me until a date, such as "decide review rounds yourself until the end of the month". Each rule names its date, its kinds, and the command that records it. `fleet proof standing` takes only a command that my instructions hold word for word.

- Record each rule once per machine with its command.
- A rule lapses on its date, or when I remove it.
- Without a rule that covers a decision, ask me.
